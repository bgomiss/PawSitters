Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Paw Sitters app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account: email address and password (or your Apple or Google sign-in), your role (sitter or owner).
  • Profile: name, profile photo, title, description, age and location, if you add them.
  • Listings: title, description, dates, location (city and its map coordinates), environment, pets and photos.
  • Activity: applications, messages and photos you send, reviews and ratings, favorites, people you block and reports you file.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token for your device, to notify you about new messages, applications and reviews.
  • Technical data processed by our service providers to run and secure the app (for example app instance identifiers, IP address and diagnostic information).

We don't use your information for advertising and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, show your profile, listings and reviews to other users, deliver messages and notifications, keep the community safe (handling reports and blocks), and operate and improve the app.

Who can see it

Signed-in users can see your profile, your listings and the reviews you've received. Applications and messages are only visible to the people involved. We don't sell your information.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) to store and process data, and Apple or Google when you choose to sign in with them. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the European Union.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile › Delete account; this permanently deletes your profile, listings and their photos, applications, reviews, messages, favorites and push tokens. Reports you filed may be kept for safety purposes.

Your rights

You can access and update your information in the app, and ask us for a copy of it or to correct or delete it by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK) you may have further rights, including objecting to processing and complaining to a data protection authority.

Children

Paw Sitters is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
